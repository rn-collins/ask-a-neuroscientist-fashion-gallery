EDITORIAL STANDARD

Experience → mechanism → limit.

01 Experience

What the wearer reports: focus, safety, power, exposure, dysphoria, ease, pain, overload.

02 Mechanism

The most plausible cognitive, sensory, autonomic, motor, social-perceptual or learning process.

03 Evidence limit

What the experiment measured, what failed to replicate, and what remains interpretation.

Publication gates

  1. One answerable question.
  2. Primary source and meaningful critique or replication.
  3. No visual without canonical record and rights disposition.
  4. Causal language matches study design.
  5. Named fashion art direction.
  6. Keyboard, contrast, alt text, captions, transcripts and reduced motion.
  7. Local-first interactive privacy.
  8. Versioned corrections.
